# Design influences

LunarMarch was designed after studying these public projects in August 2026:

- [DeepSeek and Destroy](https://github.com/frozenpepper/deepseek-and-destroy): premium-parent discipline, immutable attempts, objective evidence gates, recovery, phase barriers, and bounded evidence surfaces.
- [Codex Multi-Agent Orchestration](https://github.com/omid511/codex-agent-orchestration): Luna-first roles, reusable lanes, risk-based escalation, worktree ownership, fingerprints, and compact receipts.
- [Codex Luna Swarm](https://github.com/s-smits/codex-luna-swarm): named fan-out manifests, bounded concurrency, native/fallback transport, output collection, and read-only-by-default lanes.
- [ARC Orchestrator](https://github.com/andysolomon/arc-orchestrator): phase-aware routing, explicit task lifecycle, retry/failure classification, observability, budgets, and representative-workload evaluation.
- [Codex Luna Worker](https://github.com/antoniofulg/codex-luna-worker): simple, explicit external Luna execution through `codex exec`.

LunarMarch is an original implementation. It adopts general architectural lessons rather than copying unlicensed implementation text or code. MIT-licensed sources remain credited here; their licenses apply to their own repositories.

The intended middle ground is smaller than DSD and ARC, more durable than prompt-only orchestration, broader than a swarm launcher, and more conservative than automatic model escalation.
